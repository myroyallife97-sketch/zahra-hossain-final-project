# After hours At Shepard Hall- Choose Your Own Adventure Game
This is my final project for Intro for Computing here at CCNY, a text-based adventure game written in C++
The player is a CCNY student who wakes up from a nap to find themselves at Shepard Hall late at night.
They begin to get creeped out by their surroundings, and through a series of suspenseful, off-putting events, discovers a portal between worlds leading to a medieval fantasy world based on Game of Thrones. Players must choose a role, collect useful items, and make wise decisions to get back home to NYC. Will they make it? You decide.
